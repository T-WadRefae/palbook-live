Past Perfect

before · after · as soon as · by the time

Level 3 — Time Connectors

Name: ______________Date: ______________
1
Definition · التعريف

نربط حدثين ماضيين بروابط: before, after, as soon as, by the time — الأسبق غالبًا ماضٍ تام.We link two past actions with before, after, as soon as, by the time.

by the time / as soon as + past simple  …  had + V3
2
Key Facts · حقائق أساسية
  • by the time = بحلول ذلك الوقت كان الحدث الآخر قد اكتمل (ماضٍ تام).by the time = by then the other action had already finished.
  • as soon as = حالما / فور (حدثان متتابعان مباشرةً، غالبًا ماضٍ بسيط).as soon as = immediately after (usually both past simple).
  • after + ماضٍ تام (الأسبق) · before + ماضٍ بسيط (الأحدث).after + past perfect (earlier); before + past simple (later).
  • فاصلة إذا بدأت الجملة بالعبارة الزمنية.Use a comma when the time clause comes first.
ConnectorExample
by the time + past simpleBy the time I woke up, everyone had left.
as soon as + past simpleAs soon as the bell rang, we left.
after + past perfectAfter he had won, he took the city.
3
Steps · خطوات النجاح

by the time → الحدث الآخر had + V3. as soon as → تتابع مباشر (ماضٍ بسيط). after + تام · before + بسيط.by the time → the other action is past perfect. as soon as → immediate sequence (past simple).

by the time
By the time we got home, the rain had stopped.
as soon as
As soon as the match finished, the fans left.
4
Summary · الخلاصة
✦ تذكّر/ي دائمًا · Remember
by the time + بسيط → الآخر had + V3 · as soon as → تتابع مباشر · after/before.
