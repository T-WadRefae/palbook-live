Past Continuous

when / while — Interrupted & Parallel

Level 3 — when & while

1
Definition · التعريف

نختار بين when و while حسب الفعل: when + ماضٍ بسيط (حدث قصير)، وwhile + ماضٍ مستمر (حدث طويل جارٍ).Choose when or while by the verb: when + past simple (short), while + past continuous (long).

While + was/were V-ing  …  /  … when + past simple
2
Key Facts · حقائق أساسية
  • when + ماضٍ بسيط (القصير) · while + ماضٍ مستمر (الطويل الجاري).when + past simple (short); while + past continuous (long).
  • المعنى نفسه بصياغتين: I was reading when the phone rang = While I was reading, the phone rang.Same meaning both ways (when / while).
  • حدثان طويلان متوازيان: While I was cooking, he was cleaning.Two long parallel actions: while + past continuous for both.
  • فاصلة إذا بدأت الجملة بـ When/While.Comma when the sentence starts with When/While.
when + past simplewhile + past continuous
When the phone rang, I was reading.While I was reading, the phone rang.
I was sleeping when it happened.While I was cooking, he was cleaning.
3
Steps · خطوات النجاح

فعل قصير مفاجئ؟ → when + past simple. فعل طويل جارٍ؟ → while + was/were V-ing.Short sudden action? → when + past simple. Long ongoing action? → while + was/were V-ing.

while
While we were walking, it started to rain.
when
I was studying when my friend arrived.
متوازيان
While my mother was cooking, I was helping her.
4
Summary · الخلاصة
✦ تذكّر/ي دائمًا · Remember
when + قصير (بسيط) · while + طويل (مستمر) · فاصلة إذا بدأت بالعبارة.

Let's Practice!
1
Recognize · تعرّف

Choose the past continuous or the past simple.اختر/ي الماضي المستمر أو الماضي البسيط.

  1. While I ____ , I saw a friend. ( was walking / walked )
  2. When the teacher ____ in, we were talking. ( was coming / came )
  3. They were playing when it ____ to rain. ( was starting / started )
  4. While she ____ , the phone rang. ( was cooking / cooked )
2
Multiple Choice · اختيار من متعدد

Choose when or while.اختر/ي when أو while.

  1. ____ I was reading, the lights went out. a) Whenb) While
  2. I was sleeping ____ the earthquake happened. a) whenb) while
  3. ____ my brother was studying, I was watching TV. a) Whenb) While
3
Fill in · إكمال الفراغ

Use the past continuous for long actions and the past simple for short ones.استخدم/ي الماضي المستمر للأحداث الطويلة والبسيط للقصيرة.

  1. While we __________ (have) lunch, the guests __________ (arrive).
  2. I __________ (watch) TV when the power __________ (go) off.
  3. While my mother __________ (cook), my father __________ (water) the plants.
  4. When you __________ (call), I __________ (study) in my room.
4
Match · توصيل

Match each clause with its ending — write the letter.صِل/ي كل بداية بنهايتها — اكتب/ي الحرف.

1) While I was studying,____
2) When the rain started,____
3) I was riding my bike____
a) we were playing outside.
b) when I fell down.
c) my brother was watching TV.
