1
Definition · التعريف

في النفي والسؤال نستخدم did / didn't + الفعل المجرد (بدون ed، والشاذ يرجع لأصله).For negatives & questions we use did / didn't + the base verb (no -ed; irregulars go back to base).

(−) didn't + verb  |  (?) Did + subject + verb?  |  be: was / were
2
Key Facts · حقائق أساسية
  • النفي: didn't + base (She didn't go).Negative: didn't + base verb.
  • السؤال: Did + subject + base?Question: Did + subject + base? → Yes, I did / No, I didn't.
  • فعل الكون في الماضي.Past of "be": was (I/he/she/it) · were (you/we/they).
(+)(−)(?)
playeddidn't playDid you play?
wentdidn't goDid they go?
was / werewasn't / weren'tWas it ...?
3
Steps · خطوات النجاح

في السؤال والنفي الفعل يرجع مجرّدًا دائمًا — did وحدها تحمل زمن الماضي.In questions & negatives the main verb is always the base; "did" already shows the past.

+ / − / ?
They played. → They didn't play. → Did they play?
سؤال wh-
What did you eat yesterday?
was / were
The streets were busy and the weather was nice.
4
Summary · الخلاصة
✦ تذكّر/ي دائمًا · Remember
النفي/السؤال بـ did / didn't + فعل مجرّد؛ وفعل الكون ماضيه was / were.
