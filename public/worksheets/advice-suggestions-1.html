Giving Advice · Making Suggestions

Name: ______________Date: ______________
1
Definition · التعريف

نُعطي النصيحة (advice) لنقول ما الأفضل أن يفعله شخص، ونُقدّم الاقتراح (suggestion) لنعرض فكرة بلطف.Advice tells someone the best thing to do; a suggestion offers an idea.

Advice: should / had better  |  Suggestion: Why don't you… / What about + V-ing
2
Key Facts · حقائق أساسية
  • النصيحة: should / shouldn't + مصدر؛ والأقوى (تحذير): had better (not) + مصدر.Advice: should/shouldn't + base; stronger (warning): had better (not) + base.
  • الاقتراح: Why don't you + مصدر? · Let's + مصدر · could.Suggestions: Why don't you + base? · Let's + base · could.
  • بعد What about / How about نستخدم الفعل + ing.After What about / How about, use verb + ing.
FunctionStructureExample
Adviceshould / shouldn't + baseYou should drink water.
Advice (strong)had better (not) + baseYou'd better see a doctor.
SuggestionWhy don't you + base?Why don't you rest?
SuggestionWhat about + V-ing?What about taking a walk?
3
Steps · خطوات النجاح

نصيحة؟ → should / had better + مصدر. اقتراح؟ → Why don't you / Let's + مصدر، أو What about + ing.Advice → should/had better + base. Suggestion → Why don't you/Let's + base, or What about + ing.

نصيحة
You should eat more vegetables.
اقتراح
What about joining a sports club?
4
Summary · الخلاصة
✦ تذكّر/ي دائمًا · Remember
نصيحة → should / had better + base · اقتراح → Why don't you / Let's + base · What about + V-ing.
