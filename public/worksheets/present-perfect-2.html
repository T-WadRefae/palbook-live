Present Perfect

ever / never / already / yet / just

Level 2 — Adverbs & Signals

Name: ______________Date: ______________
1
Definition · التعريف

نستخدم ظروفًا مع المضارع التام: ever/never (الخبرة) وalready/yet/just (التوقيت) — وفرق been عن gone.Adverbs with the present perfect, and been vs gone.

Have you ever…? · never · already · not … yet · just
2
Key Facts · حقائق أساسية
  • ever في الأسئلة · never في الإثبات بمعنى النفي.ever in questions; never = not ever (in positive sentences).
  • already (قبل المتوقع) · yet (في النفي/السؤال، آخر الجملة) · just (للتو).already (sooner than expected); yet (neg/question, at the end); just (very recently).
  • been = ذهب ورجع · gone = ذهب وما زال هناك.been = went and came back; gone = went and is still there.
AdverbUseExample
everسؤالHave you ever been to Gaza?
neverنفيI have never eaten sushi.
alreadyإثباتI have already done it.
yetنفي/سؤالI haven't finished yet.
justللتوShe has just arrived.
3
Steps · خطوات النجاح

ضع/ي ever/never/already/just بين have/has والـV3، وyet في آخر الجملة.Put ever/never/already/just between have/has and V3; put yet at the end.

للتو · just
I have just finished my lunch.
خبرة · ever
Have you ever ridden a camel?
been / gone
He has gone to the market. (still there)
4
Summary · الخلاصة
✦ تذكّر/ي دائمًا · Remember
ever/never/already/just بين have والـV3 · yet بالآخر · been=رجع، gone=ما زال هناك.

Let's Practice!
1
Recognize · تعرّف

Choose the correct word.اختر/ي الكلمة الصحيحة.

  1. Have you ____ been to Jaffa? ( ever / never )
  2. I have ____ seen snow. ( ever / never )
  3. She has ____ finished her work. ( already / yet )
  4. They haven't arrived ____. ( already / yet )
2
Multiple Choice · اختيار من متعدد

Choose the correct answer.اختر/ي الإجابة الصحيحة.

  1. We have ____ eaten; we're not hungry. a) alreadyb) yetc) ever
  2. The train has ____ left — one minute ago! a) justb) yetc) never
  3. Has she finished ____? a) yetb) alreadyc) just
3
Fill in · إكمال الفراغ

Fill in with ever, never, already, yet or just.أكمِل/ي بـ ever / never / already / yet / just.

  1. I have __________ tried maqluba — it was my first time today!
  2. Have you __________ visited Jerusalem?
  3. Don't worry, I have __________ bought the bread.
  4. He hasn't called me __________.
4
been or gone · been أم gone

Choose been or gone.اختر/ي been أو gone.

  1. My father has ____ to work. (He's there now.) ( been / gone )
  2. I have ____ to Jerusalem twice. (and came back) ( been / gone )
  3. She isn't home; she has ____ to the shop. ( been / gone )
  4. Have you ever ____ to Haifa? ( been / gone )
