Past Continuous

حدثٌ كان جاريًا في الماضي — The Past Continuous

Level 1 — Foundation

Name: ______________Date: ______________
1
Definition · التعريف

الماضي المستمر لحدثٍ كان جاريًا في لحظةٍ محدّدة في الماضي (بدأ قبلها ولم ينتهِ بعد).An action that was in progress at a specific moment in the past.

was / were + verb-ing
2
Key Facts · حقائق أساسية
  • I / He / She / It was · You / We / They were + V-ing.Use was with I/he/she/it, were with you/we/they, + the -ing verb.
  • نستخدمه لحدثٍ مستمرٍّ في وقتٍ محدّد بالماضي (At 8 pm, I was studying).For an action in progress at a past time (At 8 pm, I was studying).
  • النفي: wasn't / weren't · السؤال: Was / Were + subject + V-ing?Negative: wasn't/weren't. Question: Was/Were + subject + V-ing?
Subject(+)(−)(?)
I / He / She / Itwas playingwasn't playingWas he playing?
You / We / Theywere playingweren't playingWere you playing?
3
Steps · خطوات النجاح

١) اختر/ي was / were حسب الفاعل   ٢) أضِف/ي الفعل + ing.1) choose was/were for the subject   2) add verb + ing.

في وقت محدّد
At 9 pm, I was reading a book.
مشهد ماضٍ
The children were playing in the yard.
طوال الصباح
It was raining all morning.
4
Summary · الخلاصة
✦ تذكّر/ي دائمًا · Remember
الماضي المستمر = was / were + V-ing ← حدثٌ كان جاريًا في لحظةٍ ماضية.

Let's Practice!
1
Recognize · تعرّف

Choose was or were.اختر/ي was أو were.

  1. I ____ watching TV. ( was / were )
  2. They ____ playing football. ( was / were )
  3. She ____ cooking lunch. ( was / were )
  4. You ____ sleeping early. ( was / were )
2
Multiple Choice · اختيار من متعدد

Choose the correct form.اختر/ي الصيغة الصحيحة.

  1. At 8, we were ____ dinner. a) haveb) hadc) having
  2. She was ____ a letter. a) writeb) writingc) wrote
  3. The boys ____ playing outside. a) wasb) werec) are
3
Fill in · إكمال الفراغ

Complete with was/were + the -ing verb.أكمِل/ي بـ was/were + الفعل + ing.

  1. At 7 am, I __________ (sleep).
  2. My father __________ (read) the newspaper.
  3. The students __________ (write) in their books.
  4. We __________ (wait) for the bus.
4
Match · توصيل

Match each subject with its verb phrase — write the letter.صِل/ي كل فاعل بفعله — اكتب/ي الحرف.

1) The baby____
2) My friends and I____
3) You____
a) were walking together.
b) was sleeping quietly.
c) were drawing a picture.

In Context · توظيف سياقي

Yesterday evening our house was busy. My mother (1) __________ (make) taboon bread. My father (2) __________ (water) the olive trees. My little brothers (3) __________ (play) in the yard. I (4) __________ (help) my sister with her homework. Everyone was busy!
