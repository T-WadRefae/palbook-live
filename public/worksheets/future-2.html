Level 2 — be going to

Name: ______________Date: ______________
1
Definition · التعريف

نستخدم be going to للخطط والنوايا المقررة مسبقًا، وللتنبؤ المبني على دليل.We use "be going to" for decided plans/intentions, and predictions based on evidence.

am / is / are + going to + verb
2
Key Facts · حقائق أساسية
  • am/is/are حسب الفاعل + going to + فعل مجرد.am (I) · is (he/she/it) · are (you/we/they) + going to + base.
  • النفي والسؤال نحرّك am/is/are.Negative: isn't/aren't going to. Question: Is/Are + subject + going to…?
  • التنبؤ بدليل أمامنا.Evidence prediction: Look at the clouds — it's going to rain.
  • كلمات دالّة.Signal words: tonight, tomorrow, this weekend, next week.
Subjectbe going toExample
Iam going toI am going to study.
He / She / Itis going toShe is going to cook.
You / We / Theyare going toThey are going to travel.
3
Steps · خطوات النجاح

خطة مقررة؟ أو دليل أمامك؟ ← استخدم/ي be going to.A decided plan, or evidence in front of you? ← use "be going to".

خطة · plan
We are going to visit Jerusalem next Friday.
دليل · evidence
Look! The glass is going to fall.
نفي · −
She isn't going to study tonight.
4
Summary · الخلاصة
✦ تذكّر/ي دائمًا · Remember
be going to = am/is/are + going to + فعل ← خطة مقررة أو تنبؤ بدليل.
