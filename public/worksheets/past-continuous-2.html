Past Continuous

الحدث المُقاطِع — Past Continuous + when

Level 2 — with when

Name: ______________Date: ______________
1
Definition · التعريف

حدثٌ طويل كان جاريًا (ماضٍ مستمر) يقطعه حدثٌ قصير وقع فجأة (ماضٍ بسيط)، ونربط بينهما بـ when.A long action in progress (past continuous) is interrupted by a short action (past simple), joined with when.

was / were + V-ing  +  when  +  past simple
2
Key Facts · حقائق أساسية
  • الحدث الطويل الجاري ← ماضٍ مستمر · الحدث القصير المُقاطِع ← ماضٍ بسيط.Long background action = past continuous; short interrupting action = past simple.
  • when يسبق الحدث القصير في الماضي البسيط.when comes before the short action in the past simple.
  • ترتيبان للجملة: [continuous + when + simple] أو [When + simple, continuous].Two orders: continuous + when + simple, OR When + simple, continuous.
  • إذا بدأت الجملة بـ When نضع فاصلة بعد العبارة الأولى.When the sentence starts with When, put a comma after the first clause.
continuous + when + simpleWhen + simple, continuous
I was sleeping when the alarm rang.When the alarm rang, I was sleeping.
She was cooking when I arrived.When I arrived, she was cooking.
3
Steps · خطوات النجاح

الخلفية الطويلة → was/were + V-ing. المقاطعة القصيرة بعد when → past simple.Long background → was/were + V-ing. Short interruption after when → past simple.

مقاطعة
I was reading when the lights went out.
مقاطعة
She was cooking when the guests arrived.
بادئة بـ When
When the teacher came in, we were talking.
4
Summary · الخلاصة
✦ تذكّر/ي دائمًا · Remember
was/were + V-ing + when + past simple ← حدثٌ طويل قطعه حدثٌ قصير.

Let's Practice!
1
Recognize · تعرّف

Choose the past continuous (long) or the past simple (short).اختر/ي الماضي المستمر (الطويل) أو البسيط (القصير).

  1. I ____ TV when she called. ( was watching / watched )
  2. The phone rang when I ____ . ( was sleeping / slept )
  3. When the bell ____ , we were leaving. ( was ringing / rang )
  4. They ____ when it started to rain. ( were playing / played )
