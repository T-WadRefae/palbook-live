Definition · التعريف

نستخدمه للتركيز على مدّة النشاط المستمر: for + مدة، since + بداية، والسؤال How long…?Focus on the duration of an ongoing activity: for + period, since + point, How long…?

How long + have/has + subject + been + V-ing?
2
Key Facts · حقائق أساسية
  • for + مدة (for three hours) · since + نقطة بداية (since 8 o'clock).for + a period; since + a starting point.
  • السؤال عن المدّة: How long have you been + V-ing?Ask about duration: How long have you been …ing?
  • استخدامان: مستمر حتى الآن، أو توقّف حديثًا وأثره ظاهر.Two uses: still continuing now, or stopped recently with a visible result.
for + periodsince + point
for two hourssince 8 o'clock
for a weeksince Monday
I have been reading for an hour.… since 5 pm.
3
Steps · خطوات النجاح

for لطول المدة · since لنقطة البداية · والسؤال يبدأ بـ How long.for = length of time; since = starting point; questions start with How long.

for
We have been living here for ten years.
since
She has been studying since 4 o'clock.
أثر ظاهر
You're out of breath! Have you been running?
4

✦ تذكّر/ي دائمًا · Remember
