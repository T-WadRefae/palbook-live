Level 2 — Forming Questions

1
Definition · التعريف

نكوّن سؤال wh- بالترتيب: الأداة + الفعل المساعد + الفاعل + الفعل.Form a wh- question: Wh- + auxiliary + subject + main verb?

Wh- + (is/are · do/does · did · will/can) + subject + verb?
2
Key Facts · حقائق أساسية
  • مع be: What are you doing?With be: What are you doing? / Where is she?
  • المضارع: do/does + فعل مجرد.Present: Where do you live? / What does he want?
  • الماضي: did + فعل مجرد.Past: What did you eat? (base verb)
  • المستقبل/القدرة: will / can.Future / ability: When will you come? / What can you see?
TenseExample question
Present (be)What are you doing?
Present (do)Where do you live?
Past (did)What did you eat?
Future (will)When will you travel?
3
Steps · خطوات النجاح

رتّب/ي: Wh- + مساعد + فاعل + فعل.Order: Wh- + auxiliary + subject + verb.

ترتيب
what / you / are / doing→What are you doing?
ترتيب
where / did / you / go→Where did you go?
مستقبل
When will they arrive?
4
Summary · الخلاصة
✦ تذكّر/ي دائمًا · Remember
Wh- + auxiliary + subject + verb? — بعد did الفعل يبقى مجرّدًا.

Let's Practice!
1
Recognize · تعرّف

Choose the correct auxiliary.اختر/ي الفعل المساعد الصحيح.

  1. What ____ you doing? ( are / do / did )
  2. Where ____ he live? ( do / does / is )
  3. What ____ you eat yesterday? ( do / did / are )
  4. When ____ you come tomorrow? ( will / did / do )
2
Multiple Choice · اختيار من متعدد

Choose the correct answer.اختر/ي الإجابة الصحيحة.

  1. What ____ she want? a) doesb) doc) is
  2. Where ____ they yesterday? a) didb) werec) do
  3. What ____ you do next week? a) willb) didc) are
3
Fill in · إكمال الفراغ

Complete with is, are, do, does, did or will.أكمِل/ي بـ is / are / do / does / did / will.

  1. What __________ you reading now?
  2. Where __________ your father work?
  3. What time __________ the film start yesterday?
  4. When __________ we meet tomorrow?
4
Build · ترتيب الكلمات

Put the words in order to make a question.رتّب/ي الكلمات لتكوين سؤال.

  1. (you / where / did / go) →
  2. (does / what / want / she) →
  3. (will / when / arrive / they) →

7
In Context · توظيف سياقي

Complete the interview with the correct word.أكمِل/ي المقابلة بالكلمة الصحيحة.

Reporter: (1) __________ is your name?   Sami: Sami Khalil.
Reporter: Where (2) __________ you live?   Sami: In Hebron.
Reporter: What (3) __________ you do last weekend?   Sami: I visited my uncle.
Reporter: What (4) __________ you do next summer?   Sami: I will travel to Jaffa.
8
Create · تحدٍ إبداعي
