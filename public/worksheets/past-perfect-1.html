Past Perfect

حدثٌ وقع قبل حدثٍ ماضٍ آخر — The Past Perfect

Level 1 — Foundation

Name: ______________Date: ______________
1
Definition · التعريف

الماضي التام لحدثٍ وقع واكتمل قبل حدثٍ آخر في الماضي (أو قبل لحظةٍ محدّدة في الماضي).The past perfect is for an action completed before another past action or time.

had + past participle (verb-3)
2
Key Facts · حقائق أساسية
  • لكل الضمائر: had + V3 (I / he / we / they had finished).For every subject: had + past participle (V3).
  • يصف الحدث الأسبق عند الحديث عن حدثين ماضيين.It marks the earlier of two past actions.
  • النفي: hadn't + V3 · السؤال: Had + subject + V3?Negative: hadn't + V3. Question: Had + subject + V3?
  • كلمات دالّة: already, just, never, before, by then.Signal words: already, just, never, before, by then.
(+)(−)(?)
I had finishedI hadn't finishedHad you finished?
She had goneShe hadn't goneHad she gone?
3
Steps · خطوات النجاح

١) had (لكل الضمائر)   ٢) التصريف الثالث للفعل V3 (gone, eaten, seen…).1) had (all subjects)   2) the past participle (V3).

الأسبق
When I arrived, the train had left.
already
She had already eaten before we came.
4
Summary · الخلاصة
✦ تذكّر/ي دائمًا · Remember
الماضي التام = had + V3 ← الحدث الذي وقع أوّلًا في الماضي.

Let's Practice!
1
Recognize · تعرّف

Choose the correct past participle (V3).اختر/ي التصريف الثالث الصحيح (V3).

  1. They had ____ home. ( go / went / gone )
  2. I had ____ my lunch. ( eat / ate / eaten )
  3. She had ____ a letter. ( write / wrote / written )
  4. We had ____ that film. ( see / saw / seen )
2
Multiple Choice · اختيار من متعدد

Choose the correct word.اختر/ي الكلمة الصحيحة.

  1. When we arrived, the film ____ already started. a) hasb) hadc) was
  2. He ____ finished his work before lunch. a) hadb) hasc) have
  3. They hadn't ____ the news. a) hearb) heardc) hearing
3
Fill in · إكمال الفراغ

Complete with the past perfect (had + V3).أكمِل/ي بالماضي التام (had + V3).

  1. By 9 pm, I __________ (finish) my homework.
  2. She __________ (not / see) that film before.
  3. When the guests arrived, Mum __________ (cook) the meal.
  4. We __________ (never / visit) Jaffa before last summer.
4
Match · توصيل

Match the two halves — write the letter.صِل/ي النصفين — اكتب/ي الحرف.

1) When I got to the station,____
2) She was happy because____
3) They were hungry because____
a) they hadn't eaten all day.
b) the train had already left.
c) she had passed the exam.
