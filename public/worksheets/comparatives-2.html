مقارنة الظروف — Comparing with Adverbs

Level 2 — Adverbs

Name: ______________Date: ______________
1
Definition · التعريف

نقارن كيف نؤدّي الأفعال باستخدام الظروف (adverbs). معظم الظروف تنتهي بـ -ly ← more / most؛ والظروف القصيرة (fast, hard, early) تأخذ -er / -est.We compare how actions are done. Most -ly adverbs use more/most; short adverbs use -er/-est.

more / most + adverb  |  short: adverb-er / -est
2
Key Facts · حقائق أساسية
  • ظرف بـ -ly: quickly → more quickly → most quickly.-ly adverb: quickly → more quickly → most quickly.
  • ظرف قصير = صيغة الفعل: fast → faster، hard → harder، early → earlier.Short adverb: fast→faster, hard→harder, early→earlier.
  • than مع المقارنة · the مع التفضيل.than with comparative; the with superlative.
  • الظرف يصف الفعل (كيف يحدث)، والصفة تصف الاسم.Adverbs describe verbs (how); adjectives describe nouns.
AdverbComparativeSuperlative
quicklymore quicklymost quickly
slowlymore slowlymost slowly
fastfasterfastest
earlyearlierearliest
3
Steps · خطوات النجاح

ظرف بـ -ly؟ → more / most. ظرف قصير؟ → -er / -est. أضِف/ي than للمقارنة.-ly adverb → more/most. Short adverb → -er/-est. Add than for comparison.

قصير
Sami runs faster than Ali.
-ly
She speaks more quietly than her sister.
تفضيل
He works the hardest in the class.
4
Summary · الخلاصة
✦ تذكّر/ي دائمًا · Remember
-ly → more / most · قصير → -er / -est · than للمقارنة، the للتفضيل.
