1 Introduction · Who Did It vs. What Happened

A hook about shifting focus in a sentence — 2 to 3 minutes.

🖼️ Image / situation placeholder Add a picture of a factory assembly line or a school noticeboard with "The window was broken." — تُضاف لاحقًا

Something happened 📰 "The window was broken." — we don't say who did it! "English is spoken in more than 50 countries." — the focus is on English, not on the speakers. When we want to focus on the thing rather than the doer, we use the Passive Voice.

سؤال تحفيزي: ما الفرق بين "قرأت الكتاب" (مبني للمعلوم) و"قُرئ الكتاب" (مبني للمجهول)؟ اليوم نتعلم كيف نغيّر التركيز في الإنجليزية.

❓ "The exam was postponed." — Who postponed it? Does it matter? Why might we leave out the agent?

2 Learning Outcomes · Passive Voice

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:

  • ✅ Form the passive in Present Simple, Past Simple and Present Perfect.
  • ✅ Transform an active sentence into a passive sentence.
  • ✅ Use the agent (by + doer) when needed and omit it when not.
  • ✅ Recognise why the passive is preferred in scientific, formal and news writing.

4 Teach · Forming the Passive Voice

The formula, the tense table, and when to include the agent.

Formula: be (correct tense) + past participle

TenseActivePassive
Present SimpleThey build houses.Houses are built (by them).
Past SimpleShe wrote the letter.The letter was written (by her).
Present ContinuousThey are repairing the road.The road is being repaired.
Present PerfectHe has finished the report.The report has been finished.
Future (will)They will announce the results.The results will be announced.

Steps to transform active → passive:

  • 1️⃣ Move the object to the subject position.
  • 2️⃣ Change the verb to be + past participle (same tense).
  • 3️⃣ The original subject becomes by + agent (or drop it if unknown/obvious).
