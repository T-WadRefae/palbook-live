2 Learning Outcomes · Future: will & going to

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:

  • ✅ Use will for predictions, promises and spontaneous decisions.
  • ✅ Use going to for plans and intentions already decided.
  • ✅ Form negatives and questions with both structures.
  • ✅ Choose the correct form based on context.
  • ✅ Use future time expressions (tomorrow, next year, in 2030).
ستتعلم الفرق بين will (للتنبؤات والقرارات الفورية) وgoing to (للخطط المقررة مسبقًا).

4 Teach · will vs. going to

When to use each form and how to construct them.

will + base verb: predictions, promises, spontaneous decisions

UseExample
PredictionI think it will rain tomorrow.
PromiseI will help you, I promise!
Spontaneous decision"There's no milk." "OK, I will buy some."

going to + base verb: plans already decided, evidence-based predictions

UseExample
Planned intentionI am going to study medicine.
Evidence-based predictionLook at those clouds — it is going to rain!

Negatives & questions:

She won't (will not) come. · Will you help me? · They aren't going to travel.
