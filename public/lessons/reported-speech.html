1 Introduction · What Did He Say?

A hook about passing on information — 2 to 3 minutes.

🖼️ Image / situation placeholder Add a picture of students whispering/relaying a message or a news reporter. — تُضاف لاحقًا

Imagine your teacher left a message 📩 Your friend asks: "What did the teacher say?" You answer: "She said that we have a test tomorrow." — not "She said we has a test." When we report what someone said, we shift the verb back in time.

سؤال تحفيزي: كيف تنقل كلام شخص آخر بالعربية؟ وكيف تفعل ذلك بالإنجليزية؟ اليوم نتعلم "backshift" — الإزاحة الزمنية عند النقل.

❓ Your friend told you "I am happy." How would you tell another friend what he said?

2 Learning Outcomes · Reported Speech

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:

  • ✅ Change direct speech to indirect/reported speech.
  • ✅ Apply the tense backshift: present → past, past → past perfect.
  • ✅ Change pronouns and time expressions correctly.
  • ✅ Report statements using said / told + that.
  • ✅ Report questions using asked if / whether / question word.
ستتعلم كيف تنقل كلام الآخرين مع تحويل الأفعال والضمائر والظروف الزمنية بشكل صحيح.

4 Teach · The Tense Backshift

How verbs, pronouns, and time expressions change in reported speech.

Tense backshift table:

Direct (original)Reported (shifted back)
Present Simple: I work here.Past Simple: He said he worked there.
Present Continuous: I am studying.Past Continuous: She said she was studying.
Past Simple: I went out.Past Perfect: He said he had gone out.
will: I will come.would: She said she would come.

Pronouns shift to match the new speaker's perspective:

"I love this city." → She said she loved that city.

Reporting questions:

  • Yes/No question: "Do you like tea?" → He asked if / whether I liked tea.
  • Wh- question: "Where do you live?" → She asked where I lived.
