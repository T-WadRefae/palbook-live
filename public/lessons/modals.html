Modal Verbs — can, must, should, may

🎯 Grades 6 & 7 ⏱️ ~30 min أفعال الأوجه

1 Introduction · Can You Give Advice?

A hook about helping a friend — giving advice and rules — 2 to 3 minutes.

🖼️ Image / situation placeholder Add a picture of a student looking worried about an exam, and a friend giving advice. — تُضاف لاحقًا

Imagine your friend has an exam tomorrow 📝 What would you say? "You should study!" · "You must sleep early!" · "You can ask the teacher for help!" These small words carry big meaning — they are called modal verbs.

سؤال تحفيزي: ماذا تنصح صديقك بفعله قبل الامتحان؟ نستخدم أفعال الأوجه للنصيحة والإذن والضرورة.

❓ What should students do to be healthy and successful at school?

2 Learning Outcomes · Modal Verbs

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:

  • ✅ Use can for ability and informal permission.
  • ✅ Use must for strong obligation / certainty.
  • ✅ Use should for advice and recommendation.
  • ✅ Use may for formal permission and possibility.
  • ✅ Form negatives: can't, mustn't, shouldn't.
  • ✅ Note that modals are followed by the base verb (no -s, no -ing, no to).
ستتعلم أفعال الأوجه الأربعة الرئيسية ومعانيها — النصيحة والإذن والضرورة والاحتمال.

4 Teach · Modal Meanings & Forms

Each modal, its meaning, and its negative form.

ModalMain meaningNegativeExample
canability / informal permissioncan'tI can swim. · Can I go out?
muststrong obligation / necessitymustn't (prohibition)You must wear a seatbelt.
shouldadvice / recommendationshouldn'tYou should drink water.
mayformal permission / possibilitymay notMay I ask a question? · It may rain.

Key rule: after a modal, always use the base verb (no -s, no -to, no -ing):

✅ She should study.  ✗ She should studies.  ✗ She should to study.
