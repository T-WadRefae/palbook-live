Ordinal Numbers — 1st to 12th

🎯 Grade 5 ⏱️ ~25 min الأعداد الترتيبية

1 Introduction · What Comes First?

A hook about order and position — 2 to 3 minutes.

🖼️ Image / situation placeholder Add a picture of a race / podium: 1st, 2nd, 3rd. — تُضاف لاحقًا

Think about a race 🏁 When runners cross the finish line, who is first? Who is second? Who is last? Ordinal numbers tell us the position or order of things.

سؤال تحفيزي: من هو الأول في صفك؟ ما الشهر الثالث في السنة؟ نستخدم الأعداد الترتيبية للإجابة!

❓ What is the fifth month of the year? And the tenth?

2 Learning Outcomes · Ordinal Numbers

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:

  • ✅ Say and write ordinal numbers from 1st to 12th.
  • ✅ Understand the spelling rules (first, second, third — then + th).
  • ✅ Use ordinal numbers to talk about dates and months.
  • ✅ Use ordinal numbers to describe position and order.
ستتعلم الأعداد الترتيبية من الأول إلى الثاني عشر — وكيف تستخدمها في الكلام اليومي.

4 Teach · Ordinal Number Forms

Rules for spelling and writing ordinal numbers correctly.

General rule: add -th to the number.

CardinalOrdinalNote
1firstirregular — must memorise
2secondirregular — must memorise
3thirdirregular — must memorise
4fourthfour + th
5fifthfive → fif + th
8eightheight + h → eighth
9ninthnine → nin + th
12twelfthtwelve → twelf + th

Writing dates: We use ordinals for dates.

My birthday is on the fifth of March. · Today is the third of April.
