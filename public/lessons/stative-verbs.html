1 Introduction · What Do You Feel Right Now?

A hook about verbs of feeling and the senses — 2 to 3 minutes.

Close your eyes 👁️ What do you hear? What do you smell? What do you feel? These verbs — hear, smell, feel, love, know — describe states, not actions. They have a special grammar rule that surprises many learners.

سؤال تحفيزي: هل تقول "I am knowing the answer" أم "I know the answer"؟ لماذا؟ اليوم نتعلم أفعال الحالة التي لا تُستخدم في المستمر.

❓ Do you know the answer — or are you knowing it? Which is correct and why?

2 Learning Outcomes · Stative Verbs

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:

  • ✅ Identify the main categories of stative verbs (feelings, senses, mental states, possession).
  • ✅ Understand why stative verbs are NOT used in continuous tenses.
  • ✅ Avoid the common error of using stative verbs with -ing.
  • ✅ Recognise verbs that can be both stative and dynamic (have, think, see).
ستتعلم لماذا بعض الأفعال مثل (know, love, see) لا يمكن استخدامها في صيغة المستمر وكيف تتجنب الأخطاء الشائعة.

4 Teach · Why No -ing?

The stative verb rule, examples and the tricky dual-use verbs.

The rule: Stative verbs describe a state — not an action in progress. We do not use them in continuous tenses.

Wrong ✗Correct ✅
I am knowing the answer.I know the answer.
She is loving this song.She loves this song.
They are wanting help.They want help.

Dual-use verbs — some verbs can be stative or dynamic depending on meaning:

VerbStative meaningDynamic meaning
haveI have a car. (possession)I am having lunch. (activity)
thinkI think he's right. (opinion)I am thinking about it. (process)
seeI see what you mean. (understand)I am seeing the doctor. (appointment)
