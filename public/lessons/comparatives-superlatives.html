2 Learning Outcomes · Comparatives & Superlatives

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:

  • ✅ Form comparative adjectives (taller, more beautiful).
  • ✅ Form superlative adjectives (the tallest, the most beautiful).
  • ✅ Apply the short vs. long adjective rule correctly.
  • ✅ Use irregular forms: good → better → best / bad → worse → worst.
  • ✅ Use than in comparisons and the with superlatives.
ستتعلم كيف تقارن بين شيئين (مقارنة) أو تختار الأفضل من مجموعة (تفضيل مطلق).

4 Teach · The Comparison Rule

Short adjectives, long adjectives, and irregular forms.

Short adjectives (1–2 syllables): add -er / -est

AdjectiveComparativeSuperlative
talltaller (than)the tallest
fastfasterthe fastest
bigbiggerthe biggest
heavyheavierthe heaviest

Long adjectives (3+ syllables): use more / most

AdjectiveComparativeSuperlative
beautifulmore beautifulthe most beautiful
difficultmore difficultthe most difficult

Irregular forms — must memorise:

AdjectiveComparativeSuperlative
goodbetterthe best
badworsethe worst
Palestine is more beautiful than I can describe. · Nablus is the oldest city.
