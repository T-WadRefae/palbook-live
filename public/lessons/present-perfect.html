1 Introduction · The Bridge to Now

A hook about experiences that connect the past to the present — 2 to 3 minutes.

🖼️ Image / situation placeholder Add a picture of a completed checklist, or a person arriving home with bags. — تُضاف لاحقًا

Have you ever tasted knaafeh? 🍰 Have you finished your homework yet? These questions link the past to now — the answer still matters right now. That is exactly when we use the Present Perfect.

سؤال تحفيزي: هل أنهيت وظيفتك؟ هل زرت القدس من قبل؟ هذه الأسئلة تربط الماضي بالحاضر — هذا هو المضارع التام.

❓ Have you ever visited a place you will never forget? What have you done today?

2 Learning Outcomes · Present Perfect

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:

  • ✅ Form the Present Perfect: have / has + past participle.
  • ✅ Use just (very recently), already (sooner than expected), yet (expected but not done).
  • ✅ Talk about life experiences using ever / never.
  • ✅ Distinguish Present Perfect from Past Simple.
  • ✅ Use for and since for duration.
ستتعلم كيف تربط الماضي بالحاضر وكيف تستخدم just/already/yet/ever/never بشكل صحيح.

4 Teach · Forming the Present Perfect

Structure, time markers, and common past participles.

Form: have / has + past participle

SubjectAuxiliaryExample
I / You / We / TheyhaveI have finished my work.
He / She / IthasShe has eaten already.

Key time markers and their positions:

MarkerPositionExample
justafter have/hasI have just arrived.
alreadyafter have/hasShe has already left.
yetat the end (negative / question)I haven't finished yet. · Have you eaten yet?
ever / neverafter have/hasHave you ever visited Jericho? · I have never seen snow.

for vs. since:

I have lived here for five years. (duration) · She has studied English since 2020. (starting point)
