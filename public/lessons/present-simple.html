2 Learning Outcomes

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:

  • ✅ Use the Present Simple to talk about routines, habits and facts.
  • ✅ Add -s / -es correctly with he / she / it.
  • ✅ Make negatives and questions with do / does.
  • ✅ Use frequency adverbs (always, usually, sometimes, never).
  • ✅ Tell the difference between Present Simple and Past Simple.

4 Teach — The Rule

Explanation, examples and a visual.

When do we use it? For things that are true in general or that we do regularly.

I play football every Friday. · The sun rises in the east.

Form — the golden rule: add -s (or -es) with he / she / it.

SubjectVerbExample
I / You / We / TheyplayThey play outside.
He / She / ItplaysShe plays the piano.
He / She / It (go, do, watch)+esHe goes to school.

Negatives & questions use do / does (no -s on the main verb):

  • ➖ She does not (doesn't) play tennis.
  • ❓ Do you like tea? · Does he live in Nablus?

Frequency adverbs go before the main verb:

I always eat breakfast. · He never drinks coffee.
