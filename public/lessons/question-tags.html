Question Tags — Confirming Information

🎯 Grades 7 & 9 ⏱️ ~30 min الأسئلة الذيلية

1 Introduction · You Know This, Don't You?

A hook about confirming what we know in conversation — 2 to 3 minutes.

🖼️ Image / situation placeholder Add a picture of two students chatting — one nodding in agreement. — تُضاف لاحقًا

Listen to these sentences 🗣️
"It's a beautiful day, isn't it?"
"You're from Nablus, aren't you?"
"She doesn't like fish, does she?"
These little additions at the end are called question tags — we use them to check or confirm information.

سؤال تحفيزي: هل لاحظت أن الإنجليز يُلحقون سؤالًا صغيرًا بنهاية جملهم؟ "You speak Arabic, don't you?" — هذا هو السؤال الذيلي!

❓ "English is important, ___?" — what goes in the blank?

2 Learning Outcomes · Question Tags

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:

  • ✅ Form question tags by using the opposite auxiliary and the correct pronoun.
  • ✅ Apply the rule: positive statement → negative tag and vice versa.
  • ✅ Form tags after be, do/does/did, have, and modal verbs.
  • ✅ Use question tags naturally in spoken and written English.
ستتعلم كيف تضيف سؤالًا ذيليًا صحيحًا إلى أي جملة — وهو مهارة أساسية للحديث الطبيعي بالإنجليزية.

4 Teach · The Question Tag Rule

Two rules — opposite auxiliary, opposite polarity.

The two rules:

  • 🔵 Positive statement → negative tag
  • 🟢 Negative statement → positive tag
StatementTagFull sentence
She is a teacher.isn't she?She is a teacher, isn't she?
They play football.don't they?They play football, don't they?
He went home.didn't he?He went home, didn't he?
She can't drive.can she?She can't drive, can she?
You haven't eaten.have you?You haven't eaten, have you?

Special cases:

  • I am late, aren't I? (not "amn't I")
  • Imperatives: Open the window, will you?
