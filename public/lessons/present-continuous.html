2 Learning Outcomes · Present Continuous

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:

  • ✅ Use the Present Continuous for actions happening now and temporary situations.
  • ✅ Form sentences with am / is / are + verb-ing.
  • ✅ Make negatives and questions using the auxiliary.
  • ✅ Describe changing situations and trends.
  • ✅ Distinguish Present Continuous from Present Simple.

4 Teach · The Present Continuous Rule

When we use it and how to form it correctly.

When do we use it?

  • 🔵 Actions happening right now: She is reading a book.
  • 🟢 Temporary situations: I am staying at my uncle's house this week.
  • 🟡 Changing / developing situations: The weather is getting warmer.

Form: am / is / are + verb + -ing

SubjectAuxiliaryExample
IamI am playing football.
He / She / ItisShe is reading.
You / We / TheyareThey are studying.

Spelling rules for -ing:

  • run → running (double consonant after short vowel)
  • write → writing (drop silent -e)
  • play → playing (just add -ing)

Negatives & questions:

She is not (isn't) sleeping. · Are you listening?
