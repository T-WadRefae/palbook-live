1 Introduction · A Scene in the Past

A hook about layered past events — 2 to 3 minutes.

🖼️ Image / situation placeholder Add a picture of two events happening at different times in the past — e.g., reading when a friend arrived. — تُضاف لاحقًا

Picture this 🎬 When you arrived home yesterday, your mother was cooking. The food had already been prepared for an hour. And then the lights went out. Three events. Three different moments in the past. Three different tenses!

سؤال تحفيزي: كيف تروي قصة فيها أحداث متزامنة وأخرى سبقتها في الماضي؟ اليوم نتعلم كيف يُرتّب الإنجليز أحداث الماضي المعقدة.

❓ "When I woke up, it ___ raining." + "She left because she ___ her homework." — which tenses?

2 Learning Outcomes · Past Continuous & Perfect

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:

  • ✅ Form the Past Continuous: was / were + verb-ing.
  • ✅ Use Past Continuous for background/interrupted actions and simultaneous past actions.
  • ✅ Form the Past Perfect: had + past participle.
  • ✅ Use Past Perfect for events that happened before another past event.
  • ✅ Combine both tenses with when, while, before, after, already, just.
ستتعلم تنسيق ثلاثة أزمنة ماضية: Past Simple، Past Continuous، Past Perfect — لترتيب الأحداث بوضوح.

4 Teach · was/were + -ing & had + participle

Forms, uses, and common patterns.

Past Continuous — form: was / were + verb-ing

UseExample
Action in progress when interruptedI was reading when the door opened.
Two simultaneous past actionsWhile she was cooking, he was cleaning.
Background setting in a storyThe sun was shining; birds were singing.

Past Perfect — form: had + past participle

UseExample
Action completed before another past actionShe left before I had arrived.
Explaining the cause of a past situationHe was hungry — he hadn't eaten.
When I arrived (Past Simple), she had already left (Past Perfect). · I was walking (Past Continuous) when it started (Past Simple) to rain.
