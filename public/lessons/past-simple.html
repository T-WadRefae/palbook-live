Past Simple — Narrating Past Events

🎯 Grades 5, 6 & 8 ⏱️ ~35 min الماضي البسيط

1 Introduction · A Story from Yesterday

A hook about something that happened — a story, a memory — 2 to 3 minutes.

🖼️ Image / situation placeholder Add a picture of children on a school trip or a family event. — تُضاف لاحقًا

Do you remember yesterday? 📅 Yesterday is finished. It is in the past. To tell a story about things that are done and over, we use the Past Simple.

سؤال تحفيزي: ماذا فعلت أمس بعد المدرسة؟ شارك زميلك قصة قصيرة — بالعربية أولًا!

❓ Can you tell the class one thing you did last weekend?

2 Learning Outcomes · Past Simple

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:

  • ✅ Use the Past Simple to talk about completed events and stories.
  • ✅ Form regular past tense verbs by adding -ed.
  • ✅ Use the most common irregular past tense verbs (go → went, etc.).
  • ✅ Make negatives and questions using did / didn't.
  • ✅ Use past time expressions (yesterday, last week, ago, in 2020).
ستتعلم كيف تحكي عن أحداث انتهت في الماضي — سواء كانت منتظمة (+ ed) أو شاذة (go → went).
